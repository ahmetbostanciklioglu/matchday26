Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 June 2026

In short: MatchDay 26 does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no tracking, no analytics and no ads. Anything you configure stays on your device.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the iOS application “MatchDay 26” (the “App”) handles information. The App is provided by Ahmet Bostancıklıoğlu (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at ahmetbostancikli@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not display ads.

3. Information stored on your device

  • App preferences (e.g. onboarding completion, favourite selections) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage.
  • Optional API key: if you choose to add a free API-Football key to unlock player match ratings, it is stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device only. It is sent solely to that provider to fetch ratings and is never transmitted to us.

This information never leaves your device except as needed to call the third-party services listed below, and is removed if you delete the App.

4. Third-party services

To show live content, the App connects directly from your device to the following services. Your IP address is necessarily visible to them as part of any internet request. We do not control their practices; please review their own policies:

  • TheSportsDB — live scores, fixtures, standings, squads, stadium photos and match videos.
  • Open-Meteo — live weather for stadium locations (no personal data, no key).
  • API-Football — optional player match ratings, only if you add your own key.
  • Apple Maps (MapKit) — 3D satellite imagery of stadiums.
  • YouTube — opened in an in-app browser when you play an anthem or match highlight.

5. Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Data security & retention

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or breach. On-device data (preferences and any optional key) remains under your control and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

As we hold no personal data about you, there is no personal data to access, correct or delete on our side. You can clear all on-device data at any time by deleting the App.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email ahmetbostancikli@gmail.com.

MatchDay 26 is an independent app and is not affiliated with, authorized, or endorsed by FIFA or any official tournament organizer. All team names, badges and likenesses belong to their respective owners.
